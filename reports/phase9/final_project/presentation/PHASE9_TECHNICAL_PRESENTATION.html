Phase 9 · technical presentation · 2026-11-04

A nested-bootstrap confidence interval for the optimism-corrected ΔC

Does adding four frozen pathway scores (M2) to the clinical Cox model (M1) improve optimism-corrected discrimination of relapse-free survival in the A-19 cohort (n = 452, 136 events)?

PROVISIONAL Every ΔC_corr-derived result was calculated with the as-run implementation. The CV-seed protocol deviation has not yet been adjudicated.

Conditional on the observed 136 recurrence events and 316 censored observations in the 452-patient A-19 population.

1 · Scientific problem and objective

  • Phase 9 primary: ΔC_app = +0.0140, ΔOpt = +0.0096, so ΔC_corr = θ̂ = +0.0043.
  • No interval existed for ΔC_corr. The replicate-level arrays from Phase 9 primary were not kept, so its percentile spread of the uncorrected ΔC does not propagate the uncertainty of the optimism estimate.
  • Objective: the plan v2.1 percentile interval (primary), with basic and z₀ diagnostics; endpoint Monte-Carlo error reported separately; EP-1, H1–H6 and W1–W7 evaluated as written.

Plan: docs/PHASE9_DELTAC_INTERVAL_PLAN.md v2.1

2 · Why a nested bootstrap

  • θ̂(D) = ΔC_app(D) − ΔOpt(D). The nested bootstrap applies the same functional to each outer resample D*ₖ, so the spread of θ̂(D*) carries both the sampling and the optimism-estimation uncertainty.
  • The optimism is evaluated on D*ₖ, not on D, so the original sample does not leak into the outer layer.
  • Alternatives were rejected in the audit: the uncorrected percentile spread doesn't propagate optimism uncertainty; the jackknife needs smoothness that the C-index lacks; BCa needs a jackknife for the acceleration.

3 · Architecture

Per outer replicate k (B_out = 500)

  • Event-stratified patient bootstrap D*ₖ (136 / 316 fixed)
  • M1 and M2 fully refitted on D*ₖ, giving ΔC_app(k)
  • 100 inner event-stratified bootstraps of D*ₖ; optimism evaluated on D*ₖ; pairing-preserving exclusion
  • ΔC_corr(k) = ΔC_app(k) − ΔOpt(k)

Refitted in every fit

  • Age-spline knots; gene-level μ/σ (ddof 1); pathway-score standardisation; ridge λ by 5-fold CV (molecular block only)
  • 500 000 model fits in total; nothing cached across layers

4 · Random-seed structure and the five-run design

  • SeedSequence([2026110300, run_id]).spawn(k+1)[k] for outer k; .spawn(100)[b] for inner b. The draws are a pure function of (BASE, run_id, k, b).
  • Five independent full-size runs that differ only in seed. They measure between-run endpoint variability directly at B_out = 500. That SD rests on df = 4 (≈ 35 % relative SE).
  • Deviation Plan §8.2 gives the CV its own seed, cvseed = inner[b].spawn(2)[0]. The driver passes a fresh default_rng(ss_inner[b]), which starts in the same state as the resample generator (9 / 9 cases).

13 · Where the shift comes from

  • The outer mean of ΔC_corr exceeds θ̂ by +0.0097: +0.0083 from a larger apparent ΔC on D*ₖ, and +0.0014 from a smaller ΔOpt.
  • θ̂'s Monte-Carlo error (0.00019) cannot explain z₀. Plan §7.2 predicted the opposite direction.
  • Possible explanations (duplicate rows favouring M2; optimism evaluated on D*ₖ) are inferential and untested.

14 · Protocol deviation (unadjudicated)

  • Plan: the λ-tuning CV gets its own stream, inner[b].spawn(2)[0].
  • Driver: the CV generator starts in the same PCG64 state as the resample generator, at both the apparent and the inner level.
  • Affected: M2 λ selection, and so every ΔC_corr-derived result. Unaffected: M1 (λ = 0 in all 2 500 stored apparent fits; the inner level follows from the code path).
  • θ̂ comes from Phase 9 primary, which used a third convention (one advancing generator).
  • The effect size and direction are unknown. Nothing in these results is attributed to the deviation.

15 · Limitations

  • The CV-seed deviation; θ̂ from a different implementation; EP-1's governing estimator is unspecified.
  • A7 and A8 breach in every run. Under plan §7, a material divergence warns that neither interval should be trusted.
  • The interval is conditional on 136 events; bootstrap validity is asymptotic at n = 452; distinct-patient dilution (≈ 286 / 452 per outer sample).
  • Asymmetric penalisation makes the comparison conservative by design. The validation is internal only, and Harrell's C depends on the censoring distribution.
  • An interval quantifies uncertainty only. It establishes no clinical utility, mechanism, external validity or transportability.

16 · Conclusions

  • Computationally complete 5 × 500 × 100 replicates; H1, H2, H4, H5 and H6 pass; independent checks agree.
  • Provisional result Every 95 % interval for ΔC_corr includes 0. The percentile interval averages [-0.0092, +0.0499]. There is no evidence that M2 improves on M1.
  • Interval validity questioned by the plan's own A7 and A8 diagnostics: a bootstrap distribution centred well above θ̂.
  • Open decisions: CV-seed adjudication · the EP-1 estimator and B_out top-up · how to report an interval the diagnostics flag · θ̂ centring.
