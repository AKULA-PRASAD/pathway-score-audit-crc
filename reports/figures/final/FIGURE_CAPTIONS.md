### Fig7_discrimination

**Figure 7. Apparent versus optimism-corrected discrimination (A-19, n = 452, 136 recurrences).** Open circles: apparent Harrell's C on the fitting data. Filled circles: C after bootstrap optimism correction (5 seeds × 500 event-stratified resamples). Corrected C: M1 0.6016, M2 four-set (primary) 0.6059, five-set (sensitivity) 0.6066. M2's larger optimism removes most of its apparent advantage; the corrected difference is +0.0043 (uncertainty in Fig. 8). Monte Carlo SEs (≤ 0.00058) exclude full-data cross-validation randomness. Source: reports/phase9/execution/phase9_execution_results.json.

### Fig8_corrected_deltaC

**Figure 8. Optimism-corrected ΔC and its uncertainty (central result).** Corrected nested bootstrap, five runs that differ only in random seed (not independent cohorts or experiments), each with 750 event-stratified outer resamples × 100 inner resamples; frozen verification PASS (60/60 checks) and reproduced by an independent re-implementation within this project from the same checkpoints (not external replication). (a) Blue: 95 % percentile intervals (primary): run 0 [-0.0086, +0.0519]; run 1 [-0.0090, +0.0469]; run 2 [-0.0093, +0.0484]; run 3 [-0.0091, +0.0518]; run 4 [-0.0091, +0.0494]. All five include zero (dashed line). Black line: the pre-specified point estimate θ̂ = +0.004316. Grey: basic (solid) and z₀-only bias-corrected (dotted) intervals, diagnostics only. Their divergence from the percentile interval reflects the bootstrap distribution lying above θ̂ (warnings A7/A8). (b) Distributions of ΔC_corrected(k) per run with percentile endpoints. These are bootstrap uncertainty intervals for the corrected ΔC. They are not Wald confidence intervals, and not the historical paired out-of-original ΔC intervals, which belong to a different quantity. The intervals are conditional on the observed 136 events and 316 censored observations, and a zero-including interval does not establish equivalence. Sources: reports/phase9/corrected_rerun_report/corrected_interval_results.json; checkpoints run0–4 (read-only; percentiles re-derived and matched).

### FigS2_informative_censoring

**Figure S2. Informative censoring (S3 cohort, n = 453).** The 46 patients who died without recurrence (censored in the deposited data) were older (median 75 vs 68 years), less often stage III (32.6 % vs 38.7 %) and less often treated with adjuvant chemotherapy (23.9 % vs 42.1 %) than patients alive without recurrence. This is the profile of competing non-cancer mortality. The cause-specific hazard remains estimable, but it cannot be converted into an absolute recurrence probability; no competing-risk (Fine–Gray) or composite-endpoint analysis was performed. Source: docs/PHASE6A_ENDPOINT_RESOLUTION.md §2.3.
