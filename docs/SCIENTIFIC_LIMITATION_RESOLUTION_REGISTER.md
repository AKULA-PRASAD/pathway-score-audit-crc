# Scientific limitation-resolution register

**Date:** 8 November 2026.
**Scope:** every row of presentation slide 16 ("What the evidence does and does not show"), plus the recurrence-time unit and competing risks.
**Evidence:** the registered exploratory analyses X1–X9. See `docs/EXPLORATORY_ANALYSIS_PLAN.md` for the plan and `docs/ADDITIONAL_ANALYSES_METHODS_AND_RESULTS.md` for the results; outputs are in `reports/exploratory/`, and validation is 22/22 PASS.
**All new evidence is post-hoc exploratory, from the same data.** An independent re-implementation reproduced the key quantities.
**The primary conclusion is unchanged:** the four predefined pathway scores did not demonstrate a reproducible improvement in recurrence discrimination (corrected ΔC +0.004316; all five 95 % intervals include zero).

## Classification key

- **RESOLVED**: supported by direct and adequate evidence.
- **PARTIALLY ADDRESSED**: new evidence reduces uncertainty; important limitations remain.
- **CHARACTERISED, NOT RESOLVED**: investigated, not adequately resolved.
- **NOT MEASURED**: the necessary quantity was not measured.
- **NOT ESTABLISHED**: available evidence cannot support the proposed conclusion.
- **NOT FEASIBLE**: no defensible analysis is possible with available data.
